THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE PURPOSE

PASSING IT ON.

Coach David Terzibachian has been part of Carter and Parker's soccer journey for many years. Through Thrive Soccer Academy, he has helped them grow as players and as people, teaching them the importance of confidence, teamwork, and believing in themselves.

Now, Carter and Parker are putting those lessons into action. Alongside Coach David, they are using their love of soccer to bring young players together and support children at Casa Jardin in Tijuana.

It's about more than soccer. It's about passing on what you've learned and using it to make a difference.

OUR CAUSE

CASA JARDIN

Every child deserves to feel safe, loved, and supported.

At La Casa del Jardin in Tijuana, children affected by human trafficking are given a safe and loving home, education, medical care, counseling, and the support they need to heal and thrive.

Casa Jardin is a home for children run by International Network of Hearts, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

This is the community we are playing for.
