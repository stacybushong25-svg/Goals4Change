WHY WE STARTED

SOCCER CAN DO MORE.

We've trained with Coach David at Thrive Soccer Academy for many years. Soccer has helped us become better players, but more importantly, better people.

Now, as we begin high school, we want to use what we've learned to help others.

OUR MISSION

Use soccer to make a difference in the lives of children at La Casa del Jardín.

Together with Coach David, we host soccer clinics and events to bring young players together while raising money to support International Network of Hearts and La Casa del Jardín in Tijuana.

OUR CAUSE

LA CASA DEL JARDÍN

Every child deserves to feel safe, loved, and supported.

At La Casa del Jardín in Tijuana, children affected by human trafficking are given a safe and loving home, education, medical care, counseling, and the support they need to heal and thrive.

La Casa del Jardín is a home for children run by International Network of Hearts, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

This is the community we are playing for.
